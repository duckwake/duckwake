# Code of conduct

DuckWake follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment,
personal attacks and discriminatory language are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions or any
other project space.

To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately by email; the address is on the maintainer's
GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove comments or block participants
who break this code.
